Symptoms

So, what is astigmatism?

Astigmatism is a condition where, instead of being round, your eyes are slightly egg‑shaped.1 It usually causes blurry vision at all distances. Double vision (often called "ghosting") is also common.2 Many people see glare, halos, streaks, or starburst patterns around lights.

Compensating for astigmatism can cause headaches and eye strain. It can also make driving at night difficult or dangerous.

Most cases of astigmatism can be treated with custom-made glasses or contact lenses. Some types of astigmatism require more specialized treatments like hard contacts or eye surgery.3 These treatments can cost thousands of dollars, putting them out of reach for many people. Even with proper correction, symptoms sometimes don't go away completely. Using a screen can be uncomfortable even if vision is clear otherwise.
